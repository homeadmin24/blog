## Official Source Baseline For The Rewrite

Use only current primary sources for factual statements in this article.

### Current legal/funding baseline

1. GEG `§ 71` is still in force and still requires at least 65 percent renewable energy or unavoidable waste heat for new heating systems under the statutory framework.
2. The coalition agreement and government messaging show an intent to change the framework, but that is not the same as enacted law.
3. Heating support for residential buildings is currently handled via KfW products, not via a "check BAFA first" main process for the reader.
4. WEGs can apply via KfW for measures at Gemeinschaftseigentum.
5. CO2 costs in rental constellations are not borne "only by the landlord"; the split depends on the statutory model.

## Required Factual Corrections

### 1. Reframe the legal status section

Do not say or imply that the 65 percent rule is already gone.

Replace the current framing with:

- current law: GEG remains applicable
- political outlook: changes are politically discussed/planned
- editorial rule: planned reform may be mentioned only as "angekuendigt" or "im Koalitionsvertrag vorgesehen", never as current law unless enacted

### 2. Remove the GMG certainty language

Delete or rewrite statements that say:

- the GMG "will" replace the law on a fixed date
- the 65 percent rule has ended
- gas/oil heating is now freely installable without restrictions

If the coalition plan is mentioned at all, it must be in a short boxed section like:

"Politischer Ausblick, nicht aktuelles Recht"

and must explicitly say:

"Massgeblich fuer Verwalter ist bis zu einer Gesetzesaenderung das geltende GEG."

### 3. Correct the gas-heating guidance

The article must not tell WEGs:

- that a new gas heating system is "jederzeit ohne Einschraenkungen" possible
- that kommunale Waermeplanung is irrelevant

Instead, explain:

- the current legal decision context under the GEG
- the practical relevance of kommunale Waermeplanung
- the fossil-use end horizon in GEG `§ 72 Abs. 4`
- the difference between legal permissibility, economic sense, and WEG approval risk

### 4. Correct the funding section

The heating article should no longer instruct readers to "check BAFA availability" as the main path.

Replace with:

- main grant path: KfW heating support
- add applicant-group nuance:
  - WEG at Gemeinschaftseigentum
  - owner at Sondereigentum
  - self-user bonus logic only where actually applicable

Important nuance to keep:

- WEGs at Gemeinschaftseigentum are not simply the same as a self-using single-family-house owner
- do not suggest blanket 70 percent support for all WEG constellations

### 5. Correct the CO2 cost statement

Replace "`Vermieter traegt die CO2-Kosten (nicht Mieter!)`" with a precise statement:

- in rental cases, CO2 costs are shared according to the statutory model
- the landlord share depends on the building category or applicable rule set
- the WEG article should not oversimplify landlord/tenant cost allocation

### 6. Tighten the majority guidance

The current sentence "`einfache Mehrheit reicht`" is too broad in a heating context if cost allocation and measure type are not distinguished.

Rewrite into:

- ordinary maintenance/necessary replacement may be one case
- modernization, financing, and cost allocation can create additional decision complexity
- refer readers to the dedicated WEG decision and financing articles for details
